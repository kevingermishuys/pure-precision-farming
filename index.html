About us

Every great company starts with a vision.

Pure Precision Farming was founded by Jaco Hamman with a dream of building a better future — not only for his own family, but for farmers across South Africa. As a trusted Ag Leader Dealer, we bring world-class precision agriculture technology to South African farmers while providing the knowledge, support, and service needed to ensure every solution delivers real value in the field.

  • Trusted Ag Leader Dealer
  • Based in Ventersdorp, North West

Our goal is to make precision farming simple, practical, and accessible for every farmer. Whether you're taking your first step into precision agriculture or expanding an existing system, we are committed to helping you make confident decisions that improve efficiency, accuracy, and profitability.

  • Confident, informed decisions
  • Simple, practical technology

We offer comprehensive support, including equipment installation, calibration, technical support, repairs and maintenance, operator training, transport, consultations, and after-hours assistance. Our team also restores classic tractors, reflecting our passion for agriculture and our respect for the machinery that has shaped the industry.

  • Classic tractor restoration
  • Respect for agricultural history
