Precision spraying

Improve spraying accuracy with intelligent technologies that reduce overlap, minimize waste, and maximize every application.

Solutions

Five ways we improve every pass.

1

AI Spot Spraying

Camera-guided, AI-driven detection targets weeds precisely, cutting chemical use without cutting coverage.

2

Weed Detection

Real-time weed identification technology that tells the sprayer exactly where — and where not — to apply.

3

PWM Spray Control

Pulse width modulation keeps droplet size and application rate consistent, even as ground speed changes.

4

Variable Rate Spraying

Apply exactly what each zone of the field needs, based on prescription maps rather than a single flat rate.

5

Individual Nozzle Control

Turn nozzles on and off independently to eliminate overlap on irregular field shapes and boundaries.
